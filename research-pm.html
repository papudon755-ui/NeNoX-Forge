Research, Strategy & PM

Role 04 • Operations & Pitch Strategy

Professional toolkit: Agile, Problem Framing, Market Research, Pitch Strategy, Notion, Jira

Research, strategy, and product management connect user needs, market evidence, technical execution, and measurable outcomes. The role validates the problem, identifies the most important user segment, studies competitors and substitutes, and converts findings into a focused product opportunity.

Product management turns that opportunity into priorities, requirements, acceptance criteria, milestones, and a realistic MVP. During execution, this role maintains alignment, manages sprint timelines, clarifies trade-offs, coordinates dependencies, and removes blockers.

During a pitch, the strategy lead explains why the problem matters, why the solution is differentiated, how it can be delivered responsibly, and what evidence will define success.

The role combines curiosity, structured thinking, communication, prioritization, and disciplined follow-through.

Tools and key capabilities

  • Agile — supports short feedback cycles, iterative delivery, transparent priorities and retrospectives.
  • Problem framing — separates symptoms from root causes and defines the affected user and opportunity.
  • Market research — combines interviews, surveys, desk research, competitor analysis and experiments.
  • Notion — organizes research notes, product decisions, documentation and shared team knowledge.
  • Jira — structures epics, user stories, tasks, sprint planning, dependencies and delivery tracking.
  • Pitch strategy — creates a clear narrative covering problem, audience, solution, differentiation and impact.
  • Roadmapping — communicates sequencing, milestones, assumptions and expected outcomes.
  • Product metrics — measure adoption, retention, task success, quality, risk and progress toward goals.
